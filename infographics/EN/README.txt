Time Series Infographic Series (English - complete)
================================================

70 sheets plus 1 index, in the order of the Advanced Financial Time Series
curriculum (16 units, 4 modules).

  00_index                   Series index
  01_univariate_box_jenkins  Part 1 - Univariate series and Box-Jenkins (8)
  02_cointegration_ecm       Part 2 - Cointegration and error correction (5)
  03_volatility              Part 3 - Volatility models (6)
  04_var_multivariate        Part 4 - VAR and multivariate dynamics (4)
  05_state_space             Part 5 - State space and latent states (20)
  06_bayesian                Part 6 - Bayesian financial time series (14)
  07_deep_learning           Supplement - Deep learning for time series (13)

Each folder holds PNG/ (2160px wide, for slides and print) and SVG/ (editable vector).
The SVG keeps live text, so wording can be edited; equations are embedded as
LaTeX-rendered PNGs inside the SVG.

Layout
  Standard left-right comparison sheets: 1080 x 1570
  Workflow, taxonomy and application sheets: 1080 wide, variable height

Note
  Figures marked "illustrative" are for exposition and are not estimated values.
