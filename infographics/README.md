# Infographics

One-page visual summaries of the course, 70 sheets plus a series index, in course order.
Like `supplementary/`, this folder is the one place besides the supplementary notes that is
published in **both English and Korean** — `EN/` and `KO/` carry the same sheets, the same numbers
and the same layout.

| Folder | Part | Sheets | Units |
|---|---|---:|---|
| [`00_index`](EN/00_index/PNG/series_index_v7_en.png) | Series index | 1 | — |
| [`01_univariate_box_jenkins`](EN/01_univariate_box_jenkins/) | Part 1 — Univariate series and Box-Jenkins | 8 | U01–U04 |
| [`02_cointegration_ecm`](EN/02_cointegration_ecm/) | Part 2 — Cointegration and error correction | 5 | U07 |
| [`03_volatility`](EN/03_volatility/) | Part 3 — Volatility models | 6 | U05 |
| [`04_var_multivariate`](EN/04_var_multivariate/) | Part 4 — VAR and multivariate dynamics | 4 | U06 |
| [`05_state_space`](EN/05_state_space/) | Part 5 — State space and latent states | 20 | U08–U12 |
| [`06_bayesian`](EN/06_bayesian/) | Part 6 — Bayesian financial time series | 14 | U13–U16 |
| [`07_deep_learning`](EN/07_deep_learning/) | Supplement — Deep learning for time series | 13 | beyond the syllabus |

Korean: the same folders under [`KO/`](KO/) (file names end in `_ko`).

Each part folder holds `PNG/` (2160 px wide, for slides and print) and `SVG/` (editable vector —
text stays live; equations are LaTeX-rendered images embedded in the SVG).
Standard left–right comparison sheets are 1080 × 1570; workflow, taxonomy and application sheets
are 1080 wide with variable height. Figures marked *illustrative* are for exposition and are not
estimated values.
